📐 Maths Formula Sheet
Number & Computation

Order of operations: BIDMAS — Brackets, Indices, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction (left to right)

Percentage of a number: (percent ÷ 100) × number

Converting fraction to decimal: numerator ÷ denominator

Algebra

Solving one-step equations: do the opposite operation to both sides

Expanding brackets: a(b + c) = ab + ac

Substitution: replace the letter with its value, then calculate

Geometry & Measurement

Perimeter of a rectangle: 2 × (length + width)

Area of a rectangle: length × width

Area of a triangle: ½ × base × height

Angles in a triangle add to: 180°

Angles on a straight line add to: 180°

Angles around a point add to: 360°

Circumference of a circle: π × diameter (or 2 × π × radius)

Area of a circle: π × radius²

Statistics & Probability

Mean (average): sum of values ÷ number of values

Probability of an event: favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes

Negative Numbers

Adding a negative = subtracting: a + (−b) = a − b

Subtracting a negative = adding: a − (−b) = a + b
